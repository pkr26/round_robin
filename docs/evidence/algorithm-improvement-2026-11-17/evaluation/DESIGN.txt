EVALUATION AND VISUAL DISCUSSION IMPROVEMENT DESIGN
Date: 2026-11-17. Proposal only; product source and historical audit evidence unchanged.

Definition of best
Choose the least costly, fastest protocol among those that satisfy independently
graded correctness and usefulness requirements. Treat critical factual errors,
unsupported hard-constraint conclusions, lost objections and budget violations as
separate constraints. A large usefulness average must never compensate for these
failures. More agents, rounds or algorithmic branches are justified only when a
paired evaluation shows useful improvement under the same operating envelope.

Use a vector of outcomes rather than one opaque aggregate score:
1. Critical-defect rate across every admitted primary case, including an explicit
   failure for a dangerous unsupported recommendation.
2. Decision usefulness, graded against preregistered acceptable decisions,
   decisive caveats, evidence support and next action. An appropriate abstention
   can be useful; abstaining on every task is not automatically successful.
3. Unsupported factual/constraint assertions, unresolved required evidence and
   hard-requirement violations, reported separately from general helpfulness.
4. Conservative total exposure, admitted-run completion/timeout rates and latency.
   Never report only successful runs as though they represent all admissions.
5. Repeat instability and newly introduced regressions, with task clusters kept
   intact. Repeated outputs do not become new independent cases.

Before any funded study, choose task population, minimum useful gain, acceptable
critical-defect bound/noninferiority margin, cost ceiling and latency requirement.
These are decision-owner choices; no numerical target is implied by this design.

IMPLEMENTATION ORDER

1. Share one versioned protocol between application and evaluator (EVAL-02).
Extract a pure transition policy used by both the production controller and study
runner. Its inputs are the current stage, typed evidence/claim state, unresolved
issues, prior attempts, remaining allowance and immutable configuration. It returns
the exact next role set, request scope and stop reason. Keep I/O and durable commits
in adapters, so the research harness can exercise the same decisions offline.

Version and hash the transition policy, role instructions, schemas, evidence
selection policy, model/configuration and report derivation. Bind this manifest to
each trial. Fixed-evidence mode may disable retrieval, but should retain the same
claim-version context, supported/refuted distinctions, correction/re-audit and
report-readiness rules. Deliberate differences are named, preregistered ablations.

Acceptance: with identical response tape, clock observations and budget state,
application and evaluator produce the same stage/issue/stop trace. Cover failed
audit with and without correction allowance, missing critic, delayed provider,
changed claim and unknown usage. A final failing audit must not be omitted from
the record or transformed into a successful conclusion.

2. Make visual edits follow the approved critique (EVAL-01).
Introduce a server-created EditBundle containing parent version/hash, locked brief
hash, exact selected finding IDs, each finding's observation/region/requirement,
address-or-decline decision, proposed change, preserve rules and user intent.
Resolve IDs from the immutable saved parent review; do not ask the generator to
guess what "selected findings" means. Require reasons for explicit no-change
decisions on blocking issues. Canonically hash the bundle and save it before send.

Transmit that same bundle to the editor and child reviewers. The generator sees
parent pixels and concrete requested changes; declines become explicit constraints
on the edit. Current-round reviewer output remains hidden from the other reviewer
until independent reports finish. Preserve the distinction between shared parent
history and hidden current-round opinions.

Acceptance: an edit with only intent "address selected finding" still sends that
finding's complete meaning and disposition; changing an ID, observation, selected
scope or parent hash invalidates the saved bundle. Every generated version links
to the exact sent bundle. A finding never silently disappears during serialization.

3. Add a server-owned regression matrix (EVAL-03).
When making V2/V3, initialize rows for every unresolved material/critical parent
finding and locked requirement. Each eligible critic must return exactly one
typed disposition per assigned row: resolved, persistent, regressed, uncertain,
or explicitly declined. New observations belong in a separate list. Empty model
findings cannot empty the server matrix. Missing rows stay pending.

Expose three separate fields: pixel_review_coverage, regression_review_completeness
and unresolved_blockers. Two critics receiving pixels completes only the first.
Conservative reducer: confirmed failure/regression remains blocking; disagreement
or insufficient support remains unresolved; a deliberate decline is recorded as
accepted scope/risk, not as a successful fix. Save original judgments and append
resolution records rather than overwriting them.

Use deterministic checks where they are valid: image dimensions, artifact lineage,
required native element existence and declared behavior tests. Pixel-based spelling,
legibility and aesthetic judgments retain uncertainty; OCR is an aid, not a proof.
Stop edits on exhausted allowance, no concrete change, unresolved user preference,
or no demonstrated gain. The latest version is never automatically the best.

Acceptance: known parent blocker plus two empty child responses leaves a pending
blocker; conflicting resolved/persistent judgments remain visible; a new regression
cannot be canceled by improved appearance elsewhere; V1 remains selectable.

4. Strengthen independent task and holdout identity (EVAL-04).
Define canonical substantive-case fingerprints excluding harmless IDs, titles and
ordering while retaining all decision-relevant facts, amounts, time, requirements,
preferences and evidence. Use the same implementation for text study, release
study and visual study. Hash evidence snapshots as part of case identity. Freeze
development-case fingerprints and reject exact/alias overlap in held-out cases.

This only detects known aliases. Add human provenance/stratum checks and a case
cluster ID for shared templates, suppliers, projects or common source packets.
Clustered variants may be useful robustness tests but cannot be advertised as
independent cases. The existing title-only-copy reproduction is a required
regression. Reordering options should be a metamorphic stability test, not a new
independent decision in the primary denominator.

EVALUATION DESIGN

Use two evaluation layers:
A. Engineering: all four audit regressions, shared-policy trace parity, invariant
   and fault tests, source/claim mutation tests, reference-preserving serialization,
   deadline/spending bounds. These are inexpensive and establish executable rules.
B. Empirical quality: frozen realistic tasks, actual provider responses, independent
   human adjudication and exact cost/latency records. This requires separately
   funded execution and actual competent reviewers; no simulation substitutes.

First tune and select on development cases. Then freeze prompts, policy, models,
limits, task/evidence manifests, randomization and analysis plan. Keep the release
set untouched until selection is complete; investigating its failures converts
those cases into development material and requires fresh held-out confirmation.

Primary paired comparison:
- Strong single-provider baseline with the same evidence, deterministic checks,
  abstention rules, output contract and self-check opportunity.
- Independent multi-provider proposals plus synthesis/audit.
- Candidate targeted-discussion policy with the same per-run operating ceiling.

Randomize arm execution order within each task/repeat block to reduce order and
provider-time confounding. Record actual endpoint/model/version returned. Freeze
retrieved evidence for protocol comparisons; separately evaluate retrieval when
testing the end-to-end product. Pair primary results by the same task, not by
unrelated aggregate averages.

On development cases, use explicit ablations to learn where extra work helps:
- Same-provider independent resampling/aggregation at matched compute allowance.
- Multiple independent providers without peer debate.
- Targeted objections versus broad critique of every proposal.
- Correction/re-audit enabled versus disabled with equal maximum allowance.
- Typed evidence/issue resolution enabled versus otherwise identical protocol.
Do not run every exploratory arm on the final holdout and select the luckiest one.
If several confirmatory comparisons remain, preregister multiplicity handling.

Visual comparisons must start with identical actual initial pixels and locked brief
within a matched group. Compare generation-only, one-critic revision and multi-
critic revision, plus matched image-operation allowance where the causal question
is critic count. Track full edit/review cost, including declined/failed versions.
Grade final pixels against the rubric and initial pixels; include direct blinded
final-candidate comparisons if claiming one revision policy is better than another.
Preference, hard-regression absence and user selection are separate observations.

HUMAN GRADING AND ANALYSIS

Use domain-competent humans, trained on separate calibration cases, blinded to
provider/arm and prior grades. Balance reviewer assignment across arms and task
strata. Independently double-review all confirmatory primary cases when feasible;
at minimum preregister a random double-review allocation and require independent
second review for suspected critical defects. Preserve first judgments before
adjudication. A model judge may triage, but cannot be the sole release authority.

Record rater identities/competence/conflicts, evidence actually examined, rubric
scores, critical-defect reasons and timestamped adjudications. Inspect packets
for indirect arm clues, not just literal provider names. Avoid exposing a reviewer
to another arm's answer before the first independent judgment is committed.

Report paired per-task wins/ties/losses, absolute useful-rate difference, critical-
defect difference and per-stratum counts. Use preregistered uncertainty intervals
appropriate to the task design; task-level paired resampling must resample complete
arm/repeat clusters together. For small binary paired outcomes, report discordant
case counts and an exact paired test as a sensitivity analysis. The official
statsmodels McNemar documentation describes the exact binomial calculation using
discordant pairs: https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.contingency_tables.mcnemar.html
Report reviewer
agreement before adjudication, including raw agreement and a chance-adjusted
measure where appropriate. Do not interpret agreement as correctness.

Retain every preregistered primary outcome. Missing, failed, expired and unusable
outputs are accounted for explicitly; unknown costs remain conservative exposure.
Analyze repeat variability separately, and retain adverse repeat defects. Do not
give a repeated task more weight merely because it has more executions. Show cost
and latency distributions and attainment against all admitted trials; censoring
and timeout handling must be declared before results are inspected.

SAMPLE-SIZE LIMITS

The present 16-case exploratory design and 48-case release bookkeeping are useful
starting points, not proofs of rare-defect safety or superiority. Size the study
from the smallest useful paired gain, expected discordant-case rate, desired
precision and clustering. Estimate nuisance rates on separate development/pilot
cases; do not quietly keep collecting until a result becomes significant.

Simple mathematical illustration, not a proposed release target: under independent
representative Bernoulli cases, observing zero critical failures in n cases gives
the exact one-sided 95% upper defect-rate bound
  upper = 1 - 0.05^(1/n).
With n=16 the bound is 17.07%; with n=48 it is 6.05%. At least 299 independent cases
with zero failures are needed to bring that bound below 1%. Repeated executions
of the same few cases cannot be substituted as 299 independent decisions. These
bounds do not protect against unrepresented domains or systematic shared errors.
Method reference: NIST's exact binomial confidence-limit documentation,
https://itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbino.htm .
The zero-event formula and numerical examples above were derived and calculated
locally from the binomial model; they are not empirical application results.

SELECTION AND STOP RULE

Preregister a decision rule before funded execution: candidate must meet the
critical-defect constraint, show the chosen minimum useful benefit (or an explicitly
chosen noninferiority criterion), and fit latency and conservative cost constraints.
Inspect uncertainty, strata and adverse repeats, not only a point estimate. If
evidence is inconclusive, keep the simpler established policy and report the gap.
If policies are statistically indistinguishable at the chosen precision, prefer
lower cost and shorter waits. Do not add debate rounds merely to increase apparent
sophistication. Re-evaluate after material model, prompt, policy or source changes.

official NIST and statsmodels statistical documentation checked on 2026-11-17.
