EVIDENCE AND DECISION ALGORITHM IMPROVEMENT SPECIFICATION
2026-11-17. Design only; no product code changed or tests rerun.

Objective
Improve the reliability of agent discussion by making support, disagreement and
unresolved work explicit and machine-checkable. More agents, longer debate and
agreement are not evidence of truth. Prefer additional structure where it closes
a demonstrated failure mode, with measurable improvements on unseen cases.

1. SIGNED CLAIM ASSESSMENTS, NOT REACHABILITY AS TRUTH (E-F03)

Keep the existing immutable scoped IDs, hashes and cycle checks. Replace the
single supported bit inferred from an ancestry path with immutable assessments
about a particular assertion version, scope and evidence relationship.

Minimum records:
  Assertion: id, version, kind, subject_id, predicate, typed_value, unit,
    scope, effective_interval, prose, predecessor_id.
  RelationAssessment: id, assertion_version_id, relation=supports|refutes|context,
    evidence_spans[], premise_assertion_versions[], required_premise_sets[],
    method=exact_field_match|checked_calculation|semantic_review|user_attestation,
    author_receipt, checked_at, valid_until, review_status,
    supersedes_assessment_id, scope_limitations[].
  EvidenceSpan: evidence_id, content_hash, start, end, exact_text.
  AssessmentReview: assessment_id, reviewer_receipt, accepted|disputed|rejected,
    reason, evidence_refs, reviewed_version_hash.

author/reviewer receipts bind role, configured provider/model or human actor,
run/fence, input/output versions and time. They establish attribution, not error
independence or competence. Verify text offsets/hashes and calculator receipts
deterministically. A model's semantic judgment remains attributed and reviewable.

Do not globally mark a source as true. One page can support a price, contradict a
delivery promise and say nothing about compatibility. A relation is claim- and
scope-specific. User attestation may be an allowed basis under an explicit frozen
decision policy, but must remain labeled user-attested rather than independently
verified. Merely rereading a source or receiving a second model's agreement is
not a new factual verification event.

Identity: reuse a stable typed assertion identity for the same option/property/
scope. Models may propose free-text matches; they may not merge distinct claims
or erase contradictory versions. Do not equate text hashes with semantic identity.
For unrestricted prose, retain separate assertions and an explicit proposed
equivalence relation until accepted; mark uncertain links rather than guessing.

Reducer states:
  unassessed: no admissible assessed basis;
  provisional: eligible support exists but required review is outstanding;
  supported_for_scope: accepted supporting basis and no unresolved accepted
    counter-basis/material objection under the frozen policy;
  contested: support and counter-basis coexist, or a material support dispute
    remains unresolved;
  refuted_for_scope: accepted counter-basis and no accepted supporting basis;
  stale: the only decisive accepted basis expired or changed scope.

These states are evidence/workflow states, not probabilities or factual certainty.
No averaging of provider votes; do not assign 95% confidence to a quorum. A source
status alone cannot decide a different proposition's polarity: a refutation can
support the opposite assertion. Require explicit directed relationships.

Evaluate inference as AND within a required premise set and OR across accepted
alternative sufficient bases. Today's any-root rule is insufficient when a
conclusion needs several premises. A contradicted or stale premise cannot silently
support an identical descendant claim. New independent evidence may legitimately
change the outcome, but retain the counter-basis and its explicit disposition.
Undeclared/free-form inferences remain provisional until reviewed. Reject cycles
used as justificatory support; references for discussion can still link backward.

Implementation: bounded DAG validation/topological reduction, O(V+E); propagate
changes only through the affected dependent subgraph. Retain current 200-claim,
20-new-claim/response and 40-reference/response caps. Cache only under exact graph,
evidence, policy and scope hashes. A semantic assessor must not be a hidden extra
paid call: include it in a reserved stage or explicitly authorize its allowance.

2. AN OBJECTION LEDGER WITH REQUIRED DISPOSITIONS (E-F04)

Introduce an immutable event ledger instead of aggregating only free-form dissent.
  Objection: id, target_assertion_version|option_requirement|calculation_version,
    severity, concern, opened_by_receipt, evidence_refs, materiality_basis.
  ObjectionEvent: objection_id, response_refs, disposition, reason,
    actor_receipt, evidence_refs, target_version_hash, timestamp.
  Derived states: open, answered_pending_review, resolved, withdrawn,
    accepted_risk, reopened.

An answer is not automatically a resolution. A distinct assigned reviewer or
explicit human decision must accept the response against its target and evidence.
An unsupported critic allegation is an open question, not demonstrated failure.
User acceptance of a risk does not turn a failed hard requirement into a pass.
Preference objections can be declined with an explicit user rationale; mandatory
technical conditions remain visible. If relevant evidence or target versions
change, invalidate affected resolutions and reopen the objection.

Every synthesis and audit receives unresolved material/critical objection IDs.
It must return dispositions for each, or omitted IDs remain open server-side.
The scheduler can prioritize corrections by severity and affected requirement,
but it must preserve the original call/time/money ceilings. Exhaustion publishes
an honest limited result, never fabricated consensus or endless convergence.

Report all unresolved critical/material objections before nonmaterial dissent.
The full ledger stays addressable; a 20-item display cap must provide overflow
counts and links. Last-stage findings cannot be displaced by earlier verbosity.
Do not label an omitted proposal concern resolved just because the final audit
omitted it. Deduplicate only explicit accepted equivalences, preserving authors.
Complexity: O(objections + events); bounded event counts and keyed target indexes.

3. CONSERVATIVE CONSTRAINT REDUCTION WITH EXACT ATTRIBUTION (E-F01)

Minimum result:
  ConstraintVerdict: option_id, requirement_id, result=pass|fail|unknown|not_applicable,
    basis_assessment_ids[], counter_assessment_ids[], assessor_receipts[],
    scope, basis_quality, unresolved_objection_ids[], calculation_version_id?.

Priority rules:
  a. Validate the requirement/operator/unit/scope and deterministic calculation.
  b. A complete valid calculation governs arithmetic over its stated inputs;
     a model cannot replace it with a conflicting number.
  c. A non-arithmetic pass/fail requires an accepted claim-specific assessment.
     An auditor's unsupported fail creates unknown/contested, not fail.
  d. Accepted synthesis support plus unresolved adverse assessment is contested,
     not an arbitrary majority verdict. Retain BOTH citations and attribution.
  e. Accepted adverse evidence can establish failure only under its actual scope;
     never attach the synthesis's positive evidence to the auditor's conclusion.
  f. not_applicable needs a reason and admissible basis for applicability.

Declare no feasible option only if every considered option has at least one
accepted adverse hard-requirement result under the relevant decision scope.
Where costs are merely entered inputs, say 'No option meets the requirements using
the supplied inputs'; do not promote that to verified supplier facts. Unknown
requirements remain unresolved and must not be counted as either pass or fail.

Store both arithmetic correctness and factual input support. Exact arithmetic can
be correct while the quote is stale or its installation charge is missing.
Complexity: O(options * requirements + linked assessments); retain five-option,
20-requirement limits. Prefer this auditable reducer over learned fusion or
weighted voting, which would need calibration data and can hide correlated errors.

4. BUDGETED RETRIEVAL WITH OBJECTIVE COVERAGE (E-F02, E-G02)

Freeze SearchObjective records before spending: id, type=comparison|cost|counter-
evidence|requirement, approved_term_ids, template_ids, affected_requirement_ids,
priority, query/page/passage allocation. Keep the existing approved-public-term
privacy boundary, six-query/eight-page/24-passage limits, DNS/IP controls and total
deadline. Local passage ranking may use private requirements; outgoing queries
still use only explicitly approved public terms.

Replace 'fetch all first-query hits immediately' with a small objective scheduler:
  1. Check remaining evidence-packet space before any search authorization.
  2. Collect bounded candidate queues for distinct objectives; at most 48 hits.
  3. Reserve page opportunities for cost and counterevidence before optional
     extra comparison hits. An initial policy can allocate two pages each to
     comparison/cost/counterevidence and two to remaining gaps, then be evaluated.
  4. Select round-robin across unmet mandatory objectives, then stable lexical
     relevance/source-diversity ordering. Reassign a reservation only when its
     objective has no admissible candidates or cannot be completed in time.
  5. Stop authorizing a query when no result can be used within remaining page,
     passage, time or money limits, unless discovery-only output is explicitly
     useful and represented. Do not silently charge for unreachable acquisition.

The 2/2/2/2 allocation is a bounded starting policy, not a universal optimum.
Record actual objective coverage, attempted/failed acquisitions and unfulfilled
reservations. A second query must not appear fulfilled merely because it ran.
Origin groups and near-duplicate detection support diversity, not proof that
sources are independent: different domains can copy the same source.

Passages: split canonical extracted text at paragraph/sentence boundaries into
overlapping windows <=2000 characters, retaining exact original offsets/hashes.
Rank locally using explicit requirement/query terms, relevant section headings,
cost units and exception/exclusion/negation terms. Include targeted searches for
exceptions as well as supporting facts. Then apply bounded greedy coverage and
diversity selection across objectives and source groups within the remaining
packet budget; reserve space for counterevidence and decisive unknowns.

Matching a term is a relevance heuristic, not proof of support/refutation. Expose
full-text length/hash, selected intervals, omitted interval count and objective
coverage; do not claim full-document verification. Retain selected bytes under
the existing storage/retention policy. Do not silently introduce a full-page
archive or hidden summarization/model calls. Source tables and images unsupported
by the text extractor remain explicit limitations.

Initial ranking should be deterministic and reproducible (e.g. lexical/BM25 plus
coverage/diversity rules). Compare embeddings or rerankers on held-out evidence
recall before adding them. Avoid pretending an uncalibrated relevance score is
expected information gain or probability of truth. Learned selection is a later
optional improvement, not the prerequisite to fixing starvation/prefix selection.

Complexity: text extraction O(B), candidate scoring O(P*R), greedy bounded
selection O(K*P) with cached novelty scores; B <= eight capped one-MiB pages,
R <=20 requirements and objective tags, K <=24 selected passages. Explicitly cap
window candidates and preserve overflow disclosure. The CPU work is cheap relative
to new inference and requires no extra network recipient.

5. READINESS THROUGH EXPLICIT ACCEPTED BASIS, NOT SOURCE-WIDE PROMOTION (E-G01)

Do not simply change all user_supplied evidence to supported. Add scoped
verification/attestation commands that create immutable assessment receipts for
specific cost fields and requirement claims. A cost basis must bind amount,
currency, quantity, frequency, applicability, quote scope/validity and the exact
supporting excerpt or attestation. Supplier documents need scope-aware checks;
the presence of a dollar amount alone is insufficient. A not-applicable cost
component also needs an explicit accepted justification.

The frozen policy states acceptable basis types for this decision. A user-attested
comparison may be ready under a user-attested scope if that is explicitly the
policy; it is never represented as independent supplier verification. Policies
requiring independent verification must retain conditional status until that
work actually exists. Source retrieval and model agreement cannot waive it.

Readiness should expose separate dimensions:
  input/comparison completeness; checked arithmetic; feasibility support;
  review quorum; unresolved material objections; freshness; accepted basis scope.
Use a deterministic conjunction for ready_to_choose. A missing dimension produces
conditional or insufficient_evidence with a specific next action. no_feasible
follows the qualified failure rule above. Retain all raw dimensions and basis IDs
so the UI/report cannot imply that quorum or mathematical correctness equals truth.

Begin with an explicit human-attestation workflow plus exact arithmetic and field
matching. Add bounded independent semantic review only when calibrated against
annotated evidence and budgeted explicitly. Never block all useful output while
waiting: publish conditional comparisons with known gaps and possible next actions.

6. STAGED IMPLEMENTATION AND ACCEPTANCE

Phase A: Repair deterministic report bugs first. Preserve adverse citations and
attribution; unsupported disagreements become unknown; surface final-audit
concerns and overflow. Add explicit acquisition-objective shortfall records and
reserve fetch capacity. Keep the current provider protocol and all spending fences.

Phase B: Introduce versioned RelationAssessment/Objection records behind a policy
version. Write them alongside existing outputs and compute the new reducers in
shadow mode. Compare decisions without silently relabeling historical reports.
Old reports remain bound to their original algorithm/policy versions.

Phase C: Enable obligation-carrying synthesis/audit and scoped human attestation;
switch publication to the new deterministic reducer after invariant tests and
reviewed shadow discrepancies pass. Derive compatibility fields from the new
records, never maintain two independent sources of truth.

Phase D: Introduce objective-aware passage ranking; validate it against long-page,
late-exclusion, quoted-negation, mirrored-source, stale-quote and conflicting-
source cases. Add learned reranking only if it improves held-out recall under
the same budget without increasing unsupported conclusions.

Acceptance examples (new targeted tests, not rerunning old coverage as proof):
  E-F01: unsupported auditor fail cannot produce demonstrated failure; genuine
    accepted adverse basis retains its own citations and assessor.
  E-F02: eight first-query candidates cannot starve other mandatory objectives;
    exhausted context prevents unusable search spending; shortfalls stay visible.
  E-F03: contradicted/stale ancestor alone cannot promote an identical claim;
    a genuine opposite proposition can cite a refutation with explicit polarity;
    all required premises are checked; cycles and scope mismatches fail closed.
  E-F04: all open material IDs survive arbitrary response order and >20 findings;
    missing dispositions remain open; invalidated resolutions reopen.
  E-G01: exact scoped attestation can satisfy only the policy it actually meets;
    stale/unmatched/partially verified quotes cannot certify a ready comparison.
  E-G02: relevant tail exclusions are retrieved within the same packet budget;
    every selected span reproduces its exact text/hash/locator.

Use property/metamorphic tests for graph/order invariance, bounded resource use,
freshness and qualification preservation; independent annotated examples for
semantic judgments and evidence relevance. Assess retrieval recall of decisive
support AND counterevidence, false ready/no-feasible rates, unresolved-objection
retention, answer accuracy and cost/latency against a strong single-agent baseline.
Freeze targets and unseen cases before comparing variants; report denominators,
uncertainty and failures. No 'best' claim follows from test coverage, complexity,
synthetic unanimity or a single aggregate score.
