# Phase 0A research protocol — preparation only

No participant has been recruited or observed. Do not infer findings from these
templates or contact people without explicit authorization. RR-01/RR-02 remain
pending five consented target-user records and the documented proceed/narrow
decision. Keep identifying information outside the repository.

For each consenting individual small-business owner/operator, record a sanitized
participant token, consent date/scope, the most recent real comparison, two to
four considered options and status quo, hard constraints, ranked preferences,
the current workaround, available evidence formats, missing decisive facts,
acceptable setup effort/cost/wait, and the next likely comparable decision.
Ask whether supported text/Markdown or manually entered quote items preserve
essential conditions. Record an extraction blocker explicitly if they do not.

Use the visibly fictional prototype before consented examples are available.
Without coaching, ask each participant to save/correct the brief and then locate
the recommendation or no-choice explanation, principal caveat, exact passage or
calculation, and next action. Observe reopening a saved comparison, changing a
decisive input, a partial result and Stop not confirmed. Capture elapsed intake
time, assistance given, the participant's actual answer, observed confusion and
the resulting design action separately. Do not replace observations with model
judgments or favorable reruns.

The target is at least four of five participants saving/correcting within three
minutes (excluding document gathering) and comprehending the required report
elements unaided. Inspect keyboard and narrow-screen operation separately. A
prototype's existence or automated tests do not satisfy this gate. Rehearse local
operator setup separately from the invited-user experience; hosted users do not
need three provider accounts.

Save a dated research decision with the five sanitized record paths, evidence
format findings, unresolved assumptions, observed willingness and limitations,
task/rubric revisions, and proceed/narrow rationale. Freeze cost/wait targets
before any paid Phase 0B comparison, preserving any permitted pre-study revision.
If the workflow is inconsistent, essential evidence is unsupported, or users
cannot understand/value the result, revise the workflow before broad Phase 1
acceptance. The November 16, 2026 user instruction authorized continuing
engineering through the remaining phases while deferring these blockers. This
construction override does not count as participant evidence or a release
decision; the observations and documented proceed/narrow decision remain required.
