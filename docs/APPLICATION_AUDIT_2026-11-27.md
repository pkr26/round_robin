# Round Robin application audit

November 27, 2026 · Product, decision quality and engineering review of the supplied source snapshot

## Executive decision

**Continue developing the local comparison product. Fix decision freshness, evidence attribution and first-use failures before putting paid decisions in front of customers.** The strongest product already inside Round Robin is a small-business owner’s ability to answer “Which offer fits, what could change my choice, and what should I ask the supplier?” The fictional workshop example answers those questions more clearly than the connected product currently can.

The project has earned several things worth keeping. A draft can be saved without model spend. A run freezes its inputs and budget. The calculator distinguishes an unknown charge from zero. An interrupted command has a durable identity. An export comes from a saved version. These make it possible to build a decision tool people can revisit and challenge. The next investment should make those properties visible in the ordinary quote-to-decision journey. [Application overview](../README.md) · [Run admission](../backend/src/round_robin/api/preflight.py) · [Command receipts](../backend/src/round_robin/api/commands.py) · [Calculator](../backend/src/round_robin/research/calculator.py)

**First three changes**

1. Bind each new readiness assessment to the date its evidence applies and the exact passages its owner checked.
2. Make the first screen save an incomplete decision. Reveal quote details as the owner supplies them and give an actionable path when a quote exceeds a limit.
3. Make the report name the selected supplier or explain the tie, show the decisive constraint or cost crossover, and link directly to the evidence. Carry that answer across web, Markdown and PDF.

## Product plan and release evidence

Follow that owner from the first saved draft through confirmation of the quote lines, the named choice or decisive missing fact, and the day a revised offer arrives.
